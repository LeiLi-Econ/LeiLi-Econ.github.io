In October 2023, I competed in the national final of the National Business Elite Challenge — International Trade Simulation track and won the First Prize. The competition was co-hosted by the CCPIT Commercial Sub-Council, the China Chamber of International Commerce Commercial Chamber, the China Society of Commercial Economy, and the China Convention & Exhibition Economy Research Society. Running a simulated trade business gave me a hands-on understanding of how international trade actually works in practice. I also earned a Certified International Trade Assistant certificate along the way.
