In February 2023, my two teammates and I reached the national final of the 5th "SFLEP Cup" National College Students' Intercultural Competence Contest in Shanghai and won the Second Prize. Our team of three — Xie Wen (English), Song Jiarui (Statistics), and me (International Economics & Trade) — was one of the 40 teams that advanced from nearly 500 universities. The contest was guided by the China Education Association for International Exchange, hosted by Shanghai International Studies University, and organized by Shanghai Foreign Language Education Press. Working across three different majors taught me how to communicate across disciplines, and I went on to pass the Intercultural Competence Test (Intermediate) that May.
