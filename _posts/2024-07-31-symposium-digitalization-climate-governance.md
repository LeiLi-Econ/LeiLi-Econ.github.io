In July 2024, I presented my paper "Towards carbon neutrality through digital infrastructure: Evidence from China's prefectural-level cities" at the International Conference on Digitalization for Climate Governance and Energy Transformation in Nanchang, and received a Certificate of Outstanding Papers together with my co-author Fengxiu Zhou. This was my first conference presentation, and it taught me how to communicate research to a live academic audience and how to handle questions on the spot.
