In May 2024, I won the First Prize (undergraduate group) at the 10th National College Student Competition on Energy Economics, hosted by the Chinese Society of Optimization, Programming and Operations Research (CSOPOR), for my paper "Towards carbon neutrality through digital infrastructure: Evidence from China's prefectural-level cities" — the paper later published in *Energy Strategy Reviews*, written with my supervisor Fengxiu Zhou. The contest drew 17,994 teams from 561 universities. This paper was where I first put empirical economics into practice — it was my first exposure to panel data, the DiD framework, and the full cycle of preparing a journal submission.
