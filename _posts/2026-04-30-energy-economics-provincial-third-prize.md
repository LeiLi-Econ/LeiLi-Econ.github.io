In April 2026, I won the Third Prize in the Jiangxi provincial round of the 12th National College Student Competition on Energy Economics (graduate team) for the paper "Operationalizing China's 'Two Mountains' Concept: A Machine Learning Framework for Quantifying Ecologically Oriented Development Performance in Ecological Function Zones", with Shang Junjie and Long Jing, guided by Prof. Wen Huwei. Building a machine-learning framework to quantify ecologically oriented development taught me how to turn a policy concept into measurable indicators.
