In May 2026, I joined a rural microdata collection field trip as a field investigator for the Jiangxi Provincial Academy of Agricultural Sciences, part of a national rural micro-economic observation system led by the Chinese Academy of Agricultural Sciences. The team of seven conducted household interviews across villages in Wannian County, Shangrao. Going door to door showed me how agricultural and household data are actually collected, and how the numbers we model in the office are grounded in the realities of rural life.
