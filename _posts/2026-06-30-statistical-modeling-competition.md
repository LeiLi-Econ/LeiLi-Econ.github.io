In June 2026, my team and I won the Second Prize (graduate group) in the Nanchang University selection round of the 12th National College Student Statistical Modeling Competition (Jiangxi region). Our entry, "Predicting China's gold price under international uncertainty shocks: A machine learning and large language model approach", combined machine learning with LLM-based methods. Working with Shang Junjie and Qiao Chunyu under the guidance of Prof. Wen Huwei, I learned how to bring modern ML and LLM tools into an economic forecasting problem.
