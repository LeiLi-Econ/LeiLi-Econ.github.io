In July 2026, I presented at the 5th International Academic Conference on Digital Finance (IACDF) in Nanchang, co-hosted by Nanchang University and the Asia-Pacific Applied Economics Association, where I presented our working paper "Risk avoidance or growth nurturing? How bank FinTech affects corporate ESG rating divergence" (joint work with Huwei Wen, Xuan-Ha Nghiem, and Gary Tian). Discussing the paper with researchers working on digital finance helped me sharpen the framing of the ESG-divergence question.
