## Motivation
I got this idea from a random chat with two of my friends working in the foreign trade industry. We talked about whether the Alibaba International Station (an AI-powered foreign trade service platform) really helped their daily business. The answer was positive: "It absolutely made my work easier." But then I wondered whether this AI adoption really optimized their performance. "I don't see any real difference here." — that was the response I got from a real-life first-line foreign trader. My friends told me that their company adopts AI services only because their competitors do. Another interesting fact is that, with AI services, they expanded their export destinations significantly, but the completed transaction volume in these new destinations doesn't look good at all.

This conversation made me suspect that for most SMEs, AI adoption could encourage more audacious decisions and expose them to greater risk. Internationalization is a typical risky action and falls right into my major in college. And that's the story behind this paper.
