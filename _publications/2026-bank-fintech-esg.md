## Motivation
This is joint work with my supervisor Professor Huwei Wen, Professor Xuan-Hoa Nghiem from Vietnam National University (Hanoi), and Professor Gary Tian from Macquarie University.

Many studies argue that FinTech encourages corporate ESG performance, but we find that FinTech is a broad concept; in particular, bank FinTech may exert a completely different effect on firms than enterprise FinTech. For commercial banks, it's hard to believe they're willing to cultivate genuine ESG performance; also for profit-maximising companies, it's hard to believe they're willing to give up greenwashing. 

## Current status
This paper is currently in preparation, and an earlier version was presented at the ISAFE 2026 conference (Beijing) and the 5th International Academic Conference on Digital Finance (Nanchang, 2026). We have discovered several new pieces of evidence that may alter the former claim and reveal a rather interesting measurement issue in previous ESG divergence studies.

## Abstract
ESG rating divergence has become a significant concern affecting the effectiveness of capital market governance. This study views bank FinTech as an external debt governance mechanism and uses panel data of Chinese A-share listed companies to analyze its impact on the rating divergence of corporate environmental, social and governance (ESG). The empirical results indicate that the impact of bank FinTech on ESG divergence exhibits an inverted U-shaped pattern, first significantly expands then scales back ESG divergence on average. The debt constraint of bank FinTech exacerbates the ESG divergence effect by strengthening financing constraints and making enterprises more dependent on ordinary investments rather than ESG investments. This finding implies that bank FinTech in China generally achieves sustainable development of credit business through risk-aversion motives rather than growth-nurturing motives. Moreover, when enterprise characteristics or industry attributes hinder the weakening of the bank's risk-averse motives, the effect of bank FinTech in expanding ESG divergence is reduced. These findings suggest that bank FinTech should be more used to support enterprise growth rather than exerting debt constraint pressure. In practice, when bank FinTech reaches a certain stage, the growth-nurturing motive of banks is also strengthened.
