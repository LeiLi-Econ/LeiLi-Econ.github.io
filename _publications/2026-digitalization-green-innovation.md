In press at <i>Economic Change and Restructuring</i>; the journal article link (DOI) will be added upon publication. Using panel data on China’s ecological function zones (2012–2023), this paper studies how digitalization alleviates the mismatch between endowment structure and the prevailing innovation paradigm, accelerating green innovation through digital–green synergy and inter-regional innovation networks.
