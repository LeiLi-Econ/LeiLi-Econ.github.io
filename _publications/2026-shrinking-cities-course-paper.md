This is a **course paper** (生态经济与可持续发展, Nanchang University), written in Chinese and currently being developed toward a journal submission. Using panel data for 297 Chinese prefectural cities (2003–2024), it shows that the lower emissions of net-outflow cities reflect a selection effect: population outflow itself does not change emission trajectories. Outflow cities instead sustain industrial-transfer-undertaking through institution-driven capital deepening (fiscal transfers, promotion incentives, SOE investment), keeping industrial emissions above the market equilibrium.
